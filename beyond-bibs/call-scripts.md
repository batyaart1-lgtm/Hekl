# Phone call scripts

For Israeli companies, call first; email only as a follow-up the same day ("as we discussed…"). Licensing agents such as Anjar work through their written submission process, so just submit there. Keep each call to about 5 minutes. Have the patent number, the numbers below and a pen ready.

**Key numbers:** 500+ sold · retail about $33 (₪99) · cost about $8 (₪26) · US Patent 11,986,026, granted May 2024, in force to about 2042 · 200 finished bibs and 19 rolls of fabric · sold in 3 stores in Israel

---

## 1. Creative Group Marketing (Yehuda, in English; optional)

Their written forms are the main route. A short call helps the submission get noticed.

**Call:** 1-800-678-8972 (toll-free) or (203) 359-3500. Free consultation. Ask for Gary Ahlert, who signs their forms.

> Hi, my name is Yehuda Wachmann. I'm calling on behalf of my wife, Batya, who invented a baby product. I saw on your website that you offer a free consultation. Is this a good time for two minutes?
>
> It's called Beyond Bibs: a full-length bib for babies who are learning to crawl. It covers the chest, belly and legs, and stays in place with crossed straps on the back. It has a US utility patent, granted in 2024. We've sold over 500 in Israel at about $33 each, it costs about $8 to make, and it's never been marketed in the US.
>
> We're looking for an agent to license it, or sell it outright, to a company in the baby category. Is this the kind of product you take on?

**Ask:**
- Do you represent baby products right now? Which companies do you work with in that category?
- How does your process work after we send the forms? How long does a review take?
- How do you get paid? (Expect a share of the royalties. Be cautious of any upfront fee.)
- Can I email you today with the forms, photos and video? What's the best email or name to address it to?

**End:** "Thank you. I'll email it today and mention we spoke." Write down the name of the person you spoke to.

## 2. NINO, Israel (Batya, in Hebrew)

**Call:** find the phone number on https://nino.co.il/. Ask for the owner or the person responsible for new products (מנהלת פיתוח מוצרים).

> שלום, מדברת בתיה וואכמן. אני ממציאה של מוצר לתינוקות, סינר זחילה עם פטנט אמריקאי, ואני חושבת שהוא מתאים מאוד לקו המוצרים שלכם. יש לך שתי דקות?
>
> זה סינר באורך מלא לתינוקות זוחלים: מכסה חזה, בטן ורגליים, נסגר בגב ברצועות X, ונשאר במקום. מכרתי יותר מ-500 יחידות ב-₪99, בחנויות ובנקודות מכירה, ועלות הייצור כ-₪26. יש לו פטנט רשום בארה"ב.
>
> אני מחפשת חברה שתיקח את הייצור והשיווק, בתמורה לתמלוגים או לרכישת הזכויות. זה משהו שאתם עושים?

**Ask:**
- האם אתם לוקחים מוצרים של ממציאים חיצוניים?
- מי אצלכם מחליט על מוצרים חדשים?
- אפשר לשלוח לך היום דף מוצר וסרטון? לאיזה מייל?
- אפשר להביא דוגמה?

## Handling common answers

| They say | You answer |
|---|---|
| "Just send us an email." | "Of course. Who should I address it to, so it reaches the right person?" Get a name. |
| "We don't work with outside inventors." | "I understand. Do you know anyone in the baby category who does?" |
| "What's the price?" or "How much do you want?" | "We're open: a license with royalties, or a sale. What model usually works for you?" Let them make the first offer, then check it with your financial advisor. |
| "Margins are too tight." | "It costs about ₪26 / $8 to make in small batches and sells for ₪99 / $33. In volume the cost drops further." |
| "Call back later." | "Of course. When is a good time?" Write it down and call exactly then. |

Write down the date, the person's name, what they said and the next step in the table in business-notes.md, then send the email the same day.
