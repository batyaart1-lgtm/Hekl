# Beyond Bibs — Business Notes

Last updated: Oct 5, 2026

Background, numbers and the plan for selling or licensing Beyond Bibs. The submission pack for licensing agents is in [licensing-submission-pack.md](licensing-submission-pack.md).

## Situation

- Batya Wachmann is the inventor and **sole owner** of everything: patents, brand, stock. Lea Rinat, an early co-investor, has left the business.
- Goal: sell or license the product. Running the business (manufacturing, supply, finance, marketing) is not where Batya wants to be.
- Selling directly, hiring a marketer and approaching companies by cold email have not produced a deal.
- The most common customer comment: "why isn't this more publicly known? It's such a great product."

## Product

- **Crawling bib:** full length; covers chest, belly and legs; patented X-shaped elastic back straps; waterproof inner layer; soft absorbent outer layer. Sold at ₪99.
- **Eating bib:** sold at ₪45.

## Intellectual property

- US utility patent 11,986,026 B2, granted May 2024, 14 claims
- Israeli design registration
- Israeli patent application (pending)
- Keep the US patent maintenance fees paid: the patent is the main asset in any deal.

## Feedback from companies

**Simply Good (Eleanor), Israel:** liked the product ("שאפו על המוצר המקסים והפרקטי") but declined. Their profit margins are too low to work with a third party. The no was about the licensing model, not the product.

Lessons for future pitches:

- Lead with the price, unit cost and margin. The ~₪26 cost against a ₪99 retail price answers the "low margins" objection.
- Lead with sales, not just the patent.
- Don't claim that "no competitor can copy it"; say it is protected by the US patent.
- Ask for one specific next step: a 15-minute call, or sending samples.

## Routes to an exit

1. **Licensing agents (top pick).** Many manufacturers only accept submissions through agents they know.
   - Anjar Co. & Becker Associates: infant, preschool and toy licensing; $195 concept review fee; reply in about 2 weeks. https://www.anjar.com/inventors · https://www.beckerassociates.com/inventors
   - Creative Group Marketing (Stamford, CT): represents inventors of juvenile and baby products. https://creativegroupmarketing.com/services/
2. **Sell the whole package** (patents, brand, stock, fabric, suppliers, reviews) on Flippa or Acquire.com. Expect a modest price: one patented baby product was listed for $10,000 including $23,100 of inventory (https://flippa.com/11622360).
3. **Bib makers** that already license products, such as Crown Crafts (one of the largest US producers of bibs). These are best reached through an agent.
4. **An operating partner** (an Amazon seller, a mom-influencer or a small baby brand) runs the business for a royalty or equity share.

**Warning:** never pay an invention-promotion company thousands of dollars upfront. A small review fee, like Anjar's $195, is normal.
